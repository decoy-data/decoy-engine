### Changed (plain fit snapshots, row 24zp, 2026-11-17)

A datetime column's year ranges in a plain fit snapshot are now bottom and top coded, as numeric columns already are. Every row below the year of the K-th lowest row counts under that year, and every row above the K-th highest counts under that year (K is 10, `MIN_RELEASE_COUNT`). Before this, one row at 1850 among 1990s rows made the first range start at 1850, and the plain `min` named that year. Now the outer edges and the plain `min` and `max` come from the K-th row at each end, so moving any of the 9 lowest or 9 highest rows further out changes nothing. With 10 to 18 rows the two tails overlap and one range is released between the K-th highest and K-th lowest row's years. The snapshot schema v2 shape is unchanged.
