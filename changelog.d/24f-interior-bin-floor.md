Breaking: a plain `decoy fit` snapshot writes one numeric histogram bin when it releases an `exact_values` or `quantile_grid` block; otherwise it widens to the finest 1, 2, 5 step at which every non-empty bin holds 10 rows. It can therefore have fewer bins than `numeric_bins`. `numeric_bins` outside 1 to 1000 is refused with `quality.numeric_bins_invalid` by every snapshot writer, and above 1000 by the DP fit.

### Changed (interior numeric bin floor and numeric_bins ceiling, 24F-E2, 2026-11-20)

A plain numeric histogram no longer holds a bin of 1 to 9 rows, because the grid is one uniform global recoding level and no edge follows where rows sit. The old promise of at least `numeric_bins` bins is withdrawn: `numeric_bins` sets the finest step the search may use. The float-max fallback now writes one bin. `compute_distribution_snapshot` checks `numeric_bins` first (new `MAX_NUMERIC_BINS` and `check_numeric_bins`, new reason `quality/numeric_bins_invalid`, pending). `fit_dp_snapshot` refuses `numeric_bins` over 1000 with `dp_numeric_bins_invalid`, and its message now reads `from 2 to 1000`. `contingency_top_k` and `numeric_bins` accept numpy integers. Raw (`include_values=True`) and DP output are unchanged for valid values. Schema and policy tags stay `distribution-snapshot/v2` and `flagged-redacted/v2`. Accepted residual: the chosen level shows that the next finer step failed (plan section 2).
