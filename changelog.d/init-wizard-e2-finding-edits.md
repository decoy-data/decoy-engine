### Added (post-scan review: `finding_edits`, INIT-WIZARD E2, 2026-11-17)

`decoy_engine.validation.finding_review` adds `finding_edits(config, report, decisions)`, with `FindingDecision`, `ConfigEdit`, `ReviewDismissRefusedError` and `ReviewConflictError`. It reads a `post-scan-integrity` v2 or v3 report and returns the edits that carry out one decision per finding: at most one replacement entry per column, and one `post_scan.dismissed` entry per rejected finding, bound to the column's final strategy. Only a `warning` from a detector match (or a promoted NER finding) can be dismissed. The result does not depend on the order of the decisions. A relationship key column keeps its namespace and takes only a join-safe strategy. `decoy_engine.profiling` exports `detector_entry`. New reasons, pending: `post_scan/dismiss_refused` and `post_scan/review_conflict`.
