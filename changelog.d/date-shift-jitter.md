Breaking: date_shift output changes. It now honours `jitter_days: N` as the window `[-N, N]`; before, it was ignored and shifts used -365 to 365. `jitter_days` together with `min_days` or `max_days` is refused. Affected: the shipped disguises (ccpa, cpni, default, ferpa, gdpr, glba, hipaa, pc, pci, sox) when a client applies them; the recommendations default for iso_date, us_date and eu_date (`jitter_days: 15`); platform fixtures that use `jitter_days` (`fixtures/build_hr.py`, `build_logistics.py`, `build_retail.py`, `build_energy.py`, `build_mortgage_credit.py`, `build_insurance.py`). Configs that set `min_days` and `max_days` keep today's `int()` coercion and resulting window. New refusals are a `jitter_days` combined with either bound, a non-integer or negative `jitter_days`, and any int-coerced bound outside [-106751, 106751]. No min_days or max_days value that today's handler successfully coerces is refused for its type.
