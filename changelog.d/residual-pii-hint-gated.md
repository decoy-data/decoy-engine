### Changed (residual PII, row 24zu, 2026-11-17)

Residual PII no longer fails a `redact`, `hash` or `bucketize` column on hits from name-hint-only detectors (`mrn`, `person_name`, `first_name`, `last_name`, `address`, `cvv`, `fax_number`, `health_plan_id`, `license_num`, `device_id`, `biometric_id`) when the source comparison ran and no row kept its source value. Those detectors fire on a loose shape plus a column name, so the constant `REDACTED` or a hash output can match. The finding is now `info` at zero identity, `warning` when some rows still equal their source, and `fail` when every comparable row equals its source or at least half do (three or more rows). The messages carry no rate or value. `text_redact` columns, columns with any other detector hit, and columns with no source comparison grade as before. `NAME_HINT_GATED_DETECTORS` is a new name in `decoy_engine.detection`.
