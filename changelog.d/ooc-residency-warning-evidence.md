### Fixed (run warnings pass the evidence model, R2-ooc-warning, 2026-11-17)

The `out_of_core_caller_managed_residency` warning carried an empty `provider` and a list in `detail`, and the `orphan_fk` warning carried lists in `detail`. `EvidencePayload` refuses both, so the CLI stored no evidence record for those runs. The residency warning now names the route (`provider="out_of_core_route"`) and `detail` holds one `True` flag per caller-managed shape (`resident_source`, `no_sink`, `source_loader`) plus the fixed `message`. The `orphan_fk` warning's `parent_columns` and `child_columns` are now comma-joined strings, the same form its `column` already used. The `quality_metrics["residency"]` record is unchanged. A new test checks that every warning from the out-of-core routes and the R3 route baseline is accepted by `EvidencePayload`.
