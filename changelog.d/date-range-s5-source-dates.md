### Fixed (Checked source dates and fitted datetime range, DATE-RANGE-SENTINEL S5, 2026-11-27)

The engine now checks Arrow date and timestamp columns when it reads a source: a resident table, a loader result, every lazy batch, every opened row group, the chunked entries and the profile head. A value outside the years 1 to 9999 used to fail inside pandas with `year 10183 is out of range`, which put the year in the error text. The new error, `runtime/source_date_unrepresentable`, names the table and the column, and nothing else; the count of offending cells is on the exception object. Zoned timestamps are judged by their local calendar date. A dictionary entry that no row uses is dropped instead of refusing the run, and the entries that remain keep their order. The check covers the exported `run_native_or_oracle_chunked` entry and the pandas adapter's `run` and `run_single`.

The fitted `distribution` datetime sampler now checks its snapshot before any draw. A `min`, `max` or live year bin outside the whole-day nanosecond range (1677-09-22 to 2262-04-10) used to fail inside pandas with a message that quoted a derived date, or was silently moved into the last year. It now raises the coded `plan/generation_unbounded_column`. The partial years 1677 and 2262 are sampled correctly. The range is defined once in `decoy_engine.contracts.calendar_range`.

A column that holds a date inside a list, map, struct, union, run-end-encoded, extension or nested dictionary type is refused by its type with the new `runtime/source_date_type_unsupported`, whether its dates are inside the calendar or not. The error names the table, the column and the kind of the outermost type. Drop the column, select it out, or cast it to a plain date or text column. The same gate covers an empty source, a projected Parquet read (only the selected columns count), and the isolated runner, which checks each table under its `sources` key before it stages anything. A lazy source now reports the key it was given in `sources`, not the name it was built with. Date columns leave a chunked run over buffers of their own, so a day the check excluded cannot ride out in padding or a null slot. Importing the engine works on the declared pyarrow floor (14.0.0) again.
