### Changed (ratchet bounds, RG-E2, 2026-11-24)

Test tooling only; no engine behavior changes. `scripts/dev/ratchet_check.py` now fails a raised or dropped `max` and a lowered or dropped `min` on any baseline entry, unless an `approved_growth` row that is new in the diff names the key. The module-size, log-interpolation, locator-waiver and date-shift baselines record their ceilings as `max`. A new `sentry-limits` baseline records the scalar caps and floors the sentries read (module-size GOAL, MAX and MARGIN, the package fan-out limit, and the outbound-client and public-signature floors). It is a guard baseline: dropping a key needs an `approved_growth` row, and retiring the id needs a `[retired]` decision plus one `[retired_growth]` row per old key. An approval already on the merge base never re-arms a raise.
